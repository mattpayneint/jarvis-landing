A qualified conversation is all three of the following:

  1. A live, direct exchange, a call, a meeting, or a substantive back-and-forth. Not a like, a download, or a newsletter open.
  2. With someone who fits your target profile on the facts, asset level, check size, matter value, deal size, or price point. Not someone who might someday.
  3. Where new business was plausibly on the table, and they knew what you do.

Not counted: existing client service, conversations with peers, or time with referral sources themselves. Those matter, they're just not this number.
